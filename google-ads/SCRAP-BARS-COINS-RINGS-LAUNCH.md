## Note on “every click converts”

Phrase + exact + hard negatives + Maximize conversions maximises lead quality. No Search campaign can guarantee every click converts — this setup cuts waste (pawn, US jewelry, lab-grown, price-checkers) so spend goes to sellers ready to visit.
