# Luxury Search Keyword Research — Fine Jewellery Buyers

Goal: **top-end customers** who will visit Hatton Garden with designer / fine pieces — not cheap scrap / pawn traffic.

Sources used:
- Live account Search suggestions (Hatton Garden gold buyer, selling gold jewellery in London, etc.)
- Competitor SERPs (Hatton Jewels, Daniel Christopher — branded / diamond / private showroom)
- Site catalogue (Cartier, Tiffany, Van Cleef, Graff, Rolex, Boodles…)
- UK localisation (jewellery spelling, London / Hatton Garden)

## Quality filter (luxury vs cheap)

| Attract (bid) | Avoid (negative) |
|---|---|
| Designer / signed brands | pawn, pawnbroker |
| Fine / estate jewellery | scrap, dental, broken (cheap intent) |
| Diamond / engagement ring | cash for gold (bargain hunters) |
| Hatton Garden / private valuation | ebay, amazon, free, kit, diy |
| Luxury watch (Rolex etc.) | job, jobs, salary, course |
| London office visit | forecast, price chart, melting |
| | jewelry (US spelling) |

Keep a **small** Sell Gold London ad group for reach, but ad copy must sell **Fine Jewellery Buyers / Hatton Garden / designer brands** so scrap hunters bounce and luxury sellers click.
